Read something that changed your mind? Want to argue with one of my posts, or tell me what belongs on my shelf next? I'd love to hear from you. Book people are my kind of people.

## Reach me

- **Email:** `grumble-chief-eats@duck.com`
- **GitHub:** `https://github.com/otmnsh/`

## Recommend a book

The best reading tips come from other readers. Send me a title and a line about why it stuck with you, and it might become a future post. I'll credit you if you'd like.

## Corrections

I do my best to get every fact and quote right. If you spot an error in a post, please tell me which post and which line, and I'll fix it and credit you if you'd like.

## Response time

I'm a one-person blog with a day job, so replies can take a few days. I read everything, and I answer every note, usually with a book recommendation of my own.
