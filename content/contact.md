Got stuck on a proof that every textbook skips? Found a mistake in one of my posts? Know a piece of mathematics that deserves a better explanation than the one it has? Write to me.

## Reach me

- **Email:** `grumble-chief-eats@duck.com`
- **GitHub:** `https://github.com/otmnsh/`

## Suggest a topic

Send me a Wikipedia link or just a name, plus a line about where you got lost or what bothered you about it. Confusion is the most useful raw material I get. I will credit you if you like.

## Corrections

Mathematics tolerates no small errors, and I am an amateur. If something in a post is wrong, unclear, or claims more than it proves, tell me which post, which line, and what you think is right. I will fix it and credit you if you like.

## Response time

I'm a one-person blog with a day job, so replies can take a few days. I read everything and answer every note.
